Adds the Hα, [OIII] and [SII] layers into the broadband colour image, on linear data, before the stretch.
Use it to boost the emission of a dual-band stack against its broadband base: Hα and [SII] go into red, [OIII] into green and blue.
The **gain** sets how much is added; 0 leaves the image untouched. Nothing is clipped: give the stretch headroom.
**Continuum scale k** takes away k × the broadband plane first, offered as fitted on the stack.
Pitfall: k too high hollows the stars.
