Builds the working RGB image from the bundle layers: each channel takes a layer, with a weight and a black point (**weight · max(layer − black, 0)**).
The Palette menu (Natural, RGB, HOO, SHO, HOS, Foraxx, LRGB — Custom otherwise) fills the channels with the stack's layers, named in words; a value the stack lacks is greyed with why, never replaced.
**L** replaces the luminance at its opacity.
