Puts the stars back on the starless image that StarNet made from this one.
The star layer is what StarNet removed; its brightness is kept and its **colour** scaled from grey (0) to the original (1).
At 0.25, red giants stop turning into orange blobs.
**Presence** sets how much of the stars come back: 1 keeps them all, 0.25 leaves only a hint.
**Additive** adds the star layer back; **screen** blends it so bright cores do not clip.
