**Import.** In Data, click Import Captures… and choose a night's folder: the captures stay where they are, read-only.
**Sort and profile.** The sheet sorts lights, darks, flats and biases by night and filter, and proposes a profile for the object: keep it, pick another, or none.
**Plan.** Calibration shows the master each night will use; a missing master is named, never replaced behind your back.
**Run.** In Stacking, check the preset, then Run: Siril stacks each filter and the log shows every step.
**The stack.** The Studio opens the result: one linear master per filter, with its measurements.
**Stretch, then screens.** Stretch first (MTF, GHS or asinh), then blend Hα and OIII with the emission screens; each slider shows the value applied.
**Export.** Export writes the deliverables with the recipe that made them, so the image can be redone.
