Removes gradients and light pollution from linear data, before the stretch; the model removed stays viewable.
Pick the **engine** first (GraXpert, Siril RBF, Siril polynomial), then its strength. A missing tool is named, never replaced.
**Subtract** for gradients, **divide** for vignetting (GraXpert only). **Apply to** the image, the lines or every layer.
Pitfall: a nebula filling the field reads as background; check the model, lower the strength or degree.
Apply runs the tool; compare with the previous step.
