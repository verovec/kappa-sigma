Saturation on the stretched image, in HSL: lightness and hue stay.
**Boost** raises the colour through a lightness band: the sky's chroma noise stays protected and star cores get only part of it.
**Star-core desaturation** sends the bright cores toward white, so stars do not turn into coloured discs.
**Colour kept** blends every pixel toward its Rec. 709 luminance: 1 changes nothing.
