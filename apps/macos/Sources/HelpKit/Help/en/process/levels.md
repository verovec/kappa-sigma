Three points per channel on the histogram: set the **Shadows** first, just left of the histogram foot.
Then the **Midtones** lift or lower the body: below 0.5 brightens, above 0.5 darkens.
Set the **Highlights** last, and only to stretch a faint image: values above them become white.
Pitfall: highlights pulled into the stars clip their cores to flat white discs.
**From display stretch** makes the viewer's stretch a real step; untouched values change nothing.
