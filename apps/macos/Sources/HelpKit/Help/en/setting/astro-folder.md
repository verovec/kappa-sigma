The folder your astro data lives in: AstroStack reads it and never writes there.
- **captures**: your lights, darks, flats and biases, by target then night.
- **runs**: stacks made by another tool, opened with Open Run….
- **library**: calibration masters reused as is; **library/catalogues** holds the star catalogues of the plate solve and SPCC (`just download-catalogues --spcc` fills them under the folder chosen here).
- **fixtures**: test data. Missing folders are fine: Settings only says which exist.
