A stack takes 2 to 4 GB with its Studio history; every run adds one. This setting deals with the ones you never exported.
- **Ask at launch** (default): a sheet lists them with their size; nothing goes until Delete All.
- **Delete automatically**: they go at launch, and the welcome window says which.
A stack counts as unused from its last opening in the Studio. An exported stack and each project's newest are never offered.
