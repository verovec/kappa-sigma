Adds the Hα, [OIII] and [SII] layers into the broadband colour image, on linear data, before the stretch.
Hα and [SII] go into red, [OIII] into green and blue.
The **gain** sets how much is added; 0 leaves the image untouched. Nothing is clipped: give the stretch headroom.
**Continuum scale k** first takes away k × the broadband plane, offered as fitted on the stack; too high hollows the stars.
The **black point**, offered at the line's sky, also lowers faint emission.
