Builds the working RGB image from the bundle layers: each channel takes a layer, with a weight and a black point (**weight · max(layer − black, 0)**).
The Palette menu lists the palettes the stack's layers allow (Natural, HOO, SHO, HOS, Foraxx, LRGB, RGB — Custom otherwise) and fills the channels, named in words; a value the stack lacks is greyed with why, never replaced.
**L** replaces the luminance at its opacity.
