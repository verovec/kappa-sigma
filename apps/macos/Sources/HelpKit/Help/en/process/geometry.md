Crop, turn and flip the image and every layer with it, so Hα screens and stars stay aligned.
Draw a region in the viewer, then **Use region** fills the four crops; nothing changes until Apply.
Crops are in full-resolution pixels; quarter turns and flips are exact copies of the pixels.
A fine **Angle** resamples with the chosen interpolation; at 0 nothing is resampled.
Pitfall: an angle removes the plate solution, a crop or a quarter turn keeps it, moved.
