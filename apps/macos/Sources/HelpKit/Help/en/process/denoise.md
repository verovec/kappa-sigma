Removes noise on linear data, before the stretch: the image is split into scales of 1, 2, 4 and 8 px and each is cleaned at its own **strength**, in σ of the image's noise.
**Amount** 0 leaves the image untouched; 1 applies the whole result.
**Protect stars** keeps what stands 5 to 10 σ above the background.
The **luminance** and **colour** amounts (advanced) dose the two parts: colour noise often wants more.
