The deliverable set written from the Studio's image: final, starless and the star blends, named by their role.
Separate the stars with your StarNet (a history step); without a starless only the final image is written.
Files go to the export folder of Settings plus its sub-folders, shown before you click; Choose… changes it for one export. Read-only sources and the S3 prefixes open projects read captures from are refused. Export… is ⇧⌘E.
Done once an export is recorded: each says what was written, where and from which step.
