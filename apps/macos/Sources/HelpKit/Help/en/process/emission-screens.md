Lays the Hα, [OIII] and [SII] layers over the stretched colour image, each in its own tint.
Each layer is stretched from its stack statistics; the **black point** (in σ of its noise) hides the sky, the **star removal** opening keeps only wide nebulosity.
**Continuum subtraction** takes away k × the broadband plane, leaving the true emission.
**Opacity** sets how much a layer brightens; nothing is attenuated behind your back.
More Hα comes from more narrowband exposure, not from a stronger screen.
