Click a step of the history to change it: its panel opens with the values it was applied with.
The preview shows that step and every step after it recomputed; it stops before a host tool, which runs when you apply. While you adjust, the preview is computed reduced (a badge says so); full resolution returns at rest.
Apply Change replays the steps after it and keeps the former chain under Versions; Cancel leaves everything as it was. The eye (or ⌥-click) compares.
