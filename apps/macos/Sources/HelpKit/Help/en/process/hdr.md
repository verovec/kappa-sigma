Keeps a bright galaxy core from turning flat white once stretched; runs on linear data, before the stretch.
Only the smooth light above **sky + knee** is compressed (asinh, **softness** s): fine detail and colour ratios stay.
The **knee** and **softness** are absolute above the bundle sky, so a brighter pedestal does not move them.
**Star removal** opens out stars first, so they leave no dark ring; **σ** sets what counts as smooth.
