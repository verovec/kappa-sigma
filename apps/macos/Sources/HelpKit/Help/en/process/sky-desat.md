Removes the brown and olive mottle of the sky on linear data, without greying the objects.
The masks follow brightness relative to the sky: stars and bright extended signal keep their own colour, only the faint sky and veils are treated.
**Strength** 0 changes nothing; 1 greys the sky floor.
**Keep cool hues** flattens only the warm patches: use it on reflection fields, leave it off on emission fields.
