Curves on the stretched image: drag points, the curve passes through each without overshoot.
**RGB** acts on the three channels, **Red**, **Green**, **Blue** on one each.
**Luminance** lifts or lowers the brightness and keeps the colour differences.
**Saturation** reshapes the HSL saturation: raise the middle to colour the nebula, not the noise.
A straight curve from (0, 0) to (1, 1) changes nothing.
